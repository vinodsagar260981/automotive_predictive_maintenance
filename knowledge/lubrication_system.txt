# Lubrication System Knowledge

## Oil Temperature

Oil temperature represents the temperature of the engine lubricant.

Typical operating oil temperature is approximately 100-115°C, depending on engine design and operating conditions.

High oil temperature can be associated with:
- Excessive engine load
- Low oil level
- Oil cooler problems
- Excessive friction
- Cooling system problems

Persistent high oil temperature can reduce lubricant effectiveness.

## Oil Pressure

Oil pressure is produced by the lubrication system and provides oil flow to engine bearings and other lubricated components.

Typical oil pressure depends on engine speed and operating condition.

Low oil pressure can be associated with:
- Low oil level
- Worn engine bearings
- Oil pump problems
- Oil leakage
- Diluted or degraded oil
- Excessive engine wear

High oil pressure can be associated with:
- Blocked oil passages
- Oil pressure relief valve problems
- Incorrect lubricant viscosity

Low oil pressure should be investigated because insufficient lubrication can result in severe engine damage.

## Lubrication Diagnostics

When abnormal oil pressure or oil temperature is detected, inspect:
- Engine oil level
- Oil condition
- Oil filter
- Oil pump
- Oil passages
- Pressure sensor
- Recent maintenance history

Oil pressure should be interpreted together with engine RPM and operating conditions.

## Combined Oil Analysis

A combination of:
- Increasing oil temperature
- Decreasing oil pressure

can indicate a lubrication-system condition requiring further inspection.

The telemetry trend should be compared against the vehicle's historical behavior before identifying a specific mechanical fault.
