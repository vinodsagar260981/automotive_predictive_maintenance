# Vibration Diagnostics Knowledge

## Engine Vibration

Engine vibration represents mechanical oscillations generated during engine operation.

Abnormal engine vibration can be associated with:
- Worn or damaged engine mounts
- Engine misfire
- Ignition problems
- Unbalanced rotating components
- Mechanical imbalance

## Vibration Trend

An increasing vibration trend should be compared with the vehicle's historical operating behavior.

A single high reading does not necessarily identify the exact mechanical fault.

Increasing vibration combined with other abnormal telemetry should trigger further inspection.

## Diagnostic Areas

When abnormal engine vibration is detected, inspect:
- Engine mounts
- Rotating components
- Ignition system
- Spark plugs
- Ignition coils
- Possible mechanical imbalance

## Predictive Maintenance

The following combination can indicate an increased need for inspection:

- Increasing engine vibration
- Abnormal engine temperature
- Abnormal oil pressure
- Persistent abnormal readings over multiple telemetry records

The telemetry data should be evaluated together with vehicle history and physical inspection results.
